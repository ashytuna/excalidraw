Privacy Policy

This is a personal, self-hosted build of the open-source Excalidraw whiteboard, served at ashytuna.github.io/excalidraw.

Data stored on your device

Your drawings, images and library are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to any server operated by this site.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose "Sync with Google Drive", the app asks Google for the drive.appdata permission. With it, the app can only read and write its own hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

  • The app stores your drawing, its images and your library in that folder so you can use them on other devices.
  • Everything runs in your browser and talks to Google directly. There is no backend server, and the site owner cannot access your data.
  • The Google access token is kept in your browser's local storage and expires after about an hour.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is not shared with anyone, nor used for advertising or AI training.

You can stop syncing at any time from the app menu ("Disconnect Google Drive"). You can also revoke access and delete the app's hidden data at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.
